About Us

CafeBot Café started as a small neighborhood counter with one goal: serve honest, carefully made coffee in a place that feels like home. Today we still roast in small batches, bake fresh every morning, and greet every regular by name.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick espresso or settling in with a book, we're glad you're here.
